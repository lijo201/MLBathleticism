How to interpret these results. The closest legal spot uses the ball’s observed landing location, which is unknown before the pitch; it is an optimistic benchmark. Circle center is one specified pre-pitch position. They are modeling assumptions, not guaranteed bounds on team behavior. The pitch and swing remain unchanged. Home-run and strikeout changes are held at zero in these scenarios. Distance is a movement measure, not a direct measure of athletic ability.
Source: scorecard_results.ipynb and Outfield Results for MLB. Data through September 27, 2026; 2026 is partial. The analysis uses historical batted balls and reports modeled effects, not observed rule outcomes. Infield restrictions and roster changes are not modeled.
